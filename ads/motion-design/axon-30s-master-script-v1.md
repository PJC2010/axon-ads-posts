# Axon — "Creates, not organizes" — 30s social cut
## Master production script v1.0 · August 2026

**Deliverable:** 30-second vertical/square social video (9:16 primary, 1:1 crop-safe)
**Runtime:** 30.0s including 2.0s end-card hold
**Voiceover:** ~85 words, plain-spoken male or female read, conversational pace (~150 wpm)
**Captions:** burned-in, Geist or system sans, sentence case, white on #252a31 chip (4px corners)
**Music:** minimal, low, cool-toned. Enters at 0:04. No upbeat corporate track. Resolve at 0:28.
**Brand tokens:** bg #1c2127 · cards #2f343c · accent #00a396 · bright #13c9ba · text-on-dark #7ae1d8 · headings Roboto Slab sentence case · body Geist · numbers Geist Mono tabular-nums · 4px corners · easing cubic-bezier(0.4, 1, 0.75, 0.9), 100–180ms · no emoji · no "we"

(Roboto Slab, sentence case, white; the arrow and "creates" in #13c9ba)

**SYNC:** The word "organizes" highlights on the word "organizes"; "creates" swaps to accent color on the word "creates."

**CUT:** Hard cut on the downbeat at 0:04.0. Music enters.

## Pre-export checklist
- [ ] No emoji anywhere (captions, overlays, end card)
- [ ] No "we" — product is always "Axon", third person
- [ ] Sentence case headings; Title Case only on the CTA button
- [ ] No banned words: solutions, synergy, leverage, empower, unlock, supercharge, seamless, robust, ecosystem
- [ ] Numbers in Geist Mono tabular-nums: 201,000 · Score 94 · Grade A
- [ ] Accent reads #00a396 Axon turquoise, not generic teal — check on phone and desktop
- [ ] No photography inside product frames — UI footage only
- [ ] Demo data or blurred addresses in every capture
- [ ] Motion 100–180ms, cubic-bezier(0.4, 1, 0.75, 0.9) — no spring, no bounce
- [ ] Captions on; script reads clean muted
- [ ] Claims limited to shipped product: Harris County only, no native dialer, no national coverage implied
